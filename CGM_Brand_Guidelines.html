01 — Foundation

Brand Story & Identity

Before you design anything, understand who we are and what we stand for. Every creative decision should reflect these truths.

🏛️ Who We Are

Common Ground Ministries is a Christian after-school program that has served at-risk children in East Waxahachie, Texas since 1991. We operate out of a donated portable building and provide homework help, Biblical teaching, recreation, and daily snacks to children who need a safe place to land every afternoon.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide a structured after-school and summer program with educational, physical, and spiritual benefits to at-risk children in East Waxahachie — in a safe, Christian environment. We exist to give every child a foundation for a life of purpose, faith, and confidence.

👁️ Brand Personality

CGM is warm without being soft. Joyful without being shallow. Faith-rooted without being exclusive. Our brand feels like a caring community — vibrant, welcoming, credible, and full of genuine love for the children we serve.

🎯 Who We're Talking To

Parents and families in Ellis County. Donors, churches, and civic organizations. Volunteers. Corporate sponsors. Grant-making foundations. Our brand must speak credibly to all of them — approachable for families, professional for funders.

02 — Identity Mark

The Logo

The CGM logo is a bold, colorful paint splatter mark with the lowercase "c·g·m" letterforms. It reflects the energy, joy, and creative spirit of the children we serve. Treat it with care.

Primary Mark — Use on white or light backgrounds
Logo on Color Backgrounds
On Navy — White pill
On Teal — White pill
On Orange — White pill
💡
When placing the logo on a dark or colored background, always use a white or near-white pill/card behind it. The logo's black letterforms must remain visible at all times. Never invert or recolor the logo itself.
Clear Space
Minimum clear space = height of the letter "m" on all sides
Never crowd the logo. Give it room to breathe on every side.

📏 Minimum Sizes

Print: 1 inch wide minimum
Digital: 120px wide minimum
Favicon/App: Use splatter mark only at small sizes
Embroidery/Physical: 2 inches wide minimum

📁 File Formats

PNG (transparent): Web, social, digital overlays
PDF/SVG: Print, large format, signage
JPG (white bg): Email signatures, Word docs
Always request originals from EVOBRAND

Logo Don'ts — Never Do These
Don't rotate the logoAlways display horizontally at 0°.
Don't stretch or distortLock the aspect ratio when resizing.
Don't use in grayscaleThe full color splatter is essential to the brand.
Don't place on busy backgroundsAlways ensure contrast and legibility.
Don't use faded or low-opacityLogo must always appear at full opacity.
COMMON GROUND MINISTRIES A Christian Ministry
Don't add extra text or taglinesUse the logo as-is without additions.

Color Usage Rules

🟦 Teal #1BC4C4

  • Primary buttons and interactive elements
  • Section headers and eyebrow labels
  • Links and hover states
  • Progress indicators and highlights
  • Accent lines, dividers, borders

🟧 Orange #FF6633

  • Primary call-to-action buttons
  • Donate / Give prompts
  • Event announcements
  • Warm highlights and feature callouts
  • Footer bar accents

🟦 Navy #1A1A2E

  • Primary background (dark sections)
  • All headline text on light backgrounds
  • Navigation backgrounds
  • Logo backgrounds on dark surfaces
  • Footer backgrounds

🟣 Accents (5 colors)

  • Inside the logo splatter only (standard)
  • Section color-coding in the brand kit
  • Rainbow stripe decorative elements
  • Tag and pill components
  • Never as a dominant background color
⚠️
Accessibility: Always verify text color contrast. White on Teal passes AA for large text only — use Navy on Teal for body text. Navy on White, White on Navy, and White on Orange all pass WCAG AA at all sizes.

06 — Communication

Voice & Tone

Our words are as important as our visuals. Every sentence should feel like it came from a caring neighbor — not a corporation, and not a charity begging for pity.

✅ Our Voice Is…

  • Warm and genuine — like a trusted neighbor
  • Hopeful and forward-looking
  • Proud of the children and community
  • Grounded in faith without being preachy
  • Clear and direct — no jargon
  • Grateful, not desperate
  • Confident in the impact we make
  • Inclusive — speaks to donors AND families

❌ Our Voice Is NOT…

  • Corporate or overly formal
  • Guilt-tripping or shame-based
  • Overly religious or exclusionary
  • Desperate or needy-sounding
  • Generic nonprofit-speak ("synergize")
  • Condescending toward the families we serve
  • Vague — always be specific about impact
  • Passive voice — be active and direct
Writing Examples — Good vs. Off-Brand
✅ On-Brand

"For 33 years, kids have walked through our doors carrying the weight of a hard day — and left with lighter backpacks and fuller hearts. That's Common Ground. Help us keep these doors open. ❤️"

❌ Off-Brand

"These poor underprivileged children have nowhere to go. Without your money they will have nothing. Please donate today to help these desperate kids in need."

✅ On-Brand

"$50 per semester. That's what it costs for one child to receive daily tutoring, snacks, Biblical teaching, and a community that believes in them. Enrollment is open."

❌ Off-Brand

"We need your help ASAP! We are struggling to keep our ministry running. Time is running out. Can you please help us today before it's too late??"
