🏗️ Building on Common Ground

Our Kids Deserve More Than a 30-Year-Old Portable

⚠️ Our children currently meet in a donated portable building that is over  30 years old. It's time for something permanent.

For over three decades, Common Ground Ministries has relied on a portable building generously donated to serve East Waxahachie's children. We are grateful for every year it has served us — but the time has come to build a permanent, safe, and dignified home for the next generation of children we serve.

Our Mission

A Safe Place to Learn,
Grow, and Belong

Common Ground Ministries is a Christian outreach to children and youth in East Waxahachie, Texas. Since 1991 we have provided a safe, structured environment where children can meet their spiritual, educational, and physical needs — for only $50 per semester.

Our vision is to have a positive, lasting Christian influence on every at-risk child we serve — helping them discover their potential, grow in confidence, and build the foundation they need to succeed in school and in life.

See Our Programs
To provide a structured after-school and summer program with educational, physical, and spiritual benefits to at-risk children in East Waxahachie — in a safe, Christian environment.
📚

Educational

Supervised homework time and volunteer tutors who work one-on-one with students to keep them focused and on track in school.

🎁 Annual Event
Annual Event

Christmas with Dignity

Every year, Common Ground Ministries hosts our beloved Christmas with Dignity event — a pop-up store stocked with name-brand, new items available at a fraction of retail cost. Every family in our community deserves to celebrate the season with dignity and joy.

This event has become one of the most impactful things we do — not just giving families practical help, but reminding every parent and child that they are valued, seen, and worthy of good things.
